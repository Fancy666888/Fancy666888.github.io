Hello! I am **Jiashuo Fan**, an undergraduate student in Automation at the School of Engineering, Beijing Forestry University. This website documents my learning journey, engineering practice, and research exploration.

I am interested in robotics, embodied intelligence, machine vision, and intelligent control. My current work explores how perception, decision-making, and execution can work together in real-world systems, including collaborative control for precision assembly and blueberry maturity detection with RGB-D data acquisition.

## Research Interests

- **Robotics and embodied intelligence:** task understanding, state-machine control, visual communication, and robotic-arm collaboration
- **Machine vision and 3D perception:** object detection, maturity recognition, RGB-D data acquisition, and data-quality assessment
- **Modeling and decision-making:** Bayesian inference, MCMC, statistical analysis, and dynamic scoring mechanisms
- **More to explore...**
